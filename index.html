About

I am a researcher at the UbiNet: Ubiquitous Networked Systems Lab and an MS by Research student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, where I am advised by Prof. Sandip Chakraborty.

My research focuses on wearable devices and domain adaptation of sensor data for human activity recognition. I am interested in why activity-recognition models degrade when a sensor is worn differently, by a different person, or in a new environment, and in building models that detect that shift and adapt to it at test time while staying light enough to run on the device itself.

Before IIT Kharagpur, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Techno Main Salt Lake, Kolkata, and spent a summer as a research intern at DRDO, Pune.

Research

/ 01

Wearable human activity recognition

Recognising everyday activities from IMU and accelerometer streams on wrist, pocket and body-worn devices, with an eye on what happens outside the lab.

Related work
/ 02

Domain & test-time adaptation

Adapting sensor models to new users, sensor placements and environments without labelled target data, including source-free and online adaptation at inference time.

Related work
/ 03

Shift detection & on-device deployment

Lightweight signals for spotting distribution shift at runtime, so that adaptation is triggered only when needed and the whole pipeline fits on resource-constrained devices.
